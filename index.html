Studio

A designer who drapes before she cuts.

DIBA STUDIO is an independent fashion design practice built around one idea: a garment should be understood digitally before it is committed to fabric.

Every piece begins as a 3D render — fit, drape, and movement tested in CLO3D — then is realized by hand in fabric, pattern, and stitch. The result is design work that speaks two languages fluently: the precision of digital fashion, and the discipline of the sewing table.

The current focus is occasionwear and eveningwear, where construction and silhouette carry the most weight — and where the gap between a digital render and a real garment is felt the most.

Method

From sketch to silhouette.

01 — Sketch

Idea & concept

Silhouette, fabric story, and reference are worked out on paper before anything is built.

02 — Digital

CLO3D render

The garment is draped and simulated digitally — fit, movement, and proportion tested before a single cut is made.

03 — Physical

Pattern & sew

The digital garment is translated into a real pattern and hand-finished into the final piece.
