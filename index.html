Studio

A designer who drapes before she cuts.

DIBA STUDIO is an independent fashion design practice built on one principle: a garment should exist digitally before it exists in fabric.

Every project moves through two stages — first draped and rendered in 3D using CLO3D, then constructed by hand into a real, wearable garment. This dual process means designs are tested for fit, movement, and proportion long before fabric is cut, and every digital render has a physical counterpart to prove it works.

The result is a practice that speaks both languages of modern fashion: digital prototyping and hands-on construction — built for designers and small brands who need both.
